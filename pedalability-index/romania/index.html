Is it safe to cycle in Romania?

The 20 ranked cities in Romania average 14.3 out of 100 in the Pedalability Index 2026, against 54.9 across all 300 European cities. Higher score = lower assessed risk. Cluj-Napoca ranks highest (246th of 300, score 35.5) and Iasi lowest (299th, score 0.0). The average is a simple mean of the ranked cities, not weighted by population.

The index runs from 0 to 100 on a fixed scale. Higher score = lower assessed risk. A score of 0.0 means the lowest of the 300 cities and a score of 100 the highest; neither is an absolute level of risk.

What this score doesn't tell you

The score predicts how badly a crash ends (injury severity), not whether a crash happens.

It was checked against official crash records from major European cities in five countries. That check found the score's signal along streets and not at junctions, where much urban cycling harm happens.

The figures are associations, not causes. Nothing yet shows that changing one rider's route changes their outcome.

A city's score is an average over its whole street network. Risk changes street by street.

Questions about cycling in Romania

Which city in Romania is lowest-risk for cycling?

Cluj-Napoca, on this measure. It ranks 246th of 300 European cities in the Pedalability Index 2026, with a score of 35.5 out of 100. Higher score = lower assessed risk.

Does a high score mean a city is safe to cycle in?

No. The score compares cities with each other. It predicts how badly a crash ends (injury severity), not whether one happens, and it has no signal at junctions. A score of 0.0 means the lowest of the 300 cities, not an absence of safety.
