Is it safe to cycle in Paris?

Paris ranks 13th of 300 European cities in the Pedalability Index 2026, with a score of 93.7 out of 100 (likely rank 6th–19th). Higher score = lower assessed risk. About 6.0% of its street length rates High risk for cyclists, against 10.9% in Lyon (26th). Risk changes street by street, so the route you choose matters more than the city average.

Paris's riskiest main roads aren't in the centre: they form a ring. Almost all of the Boulevards des Maréchaux, the ring road just inside the Périphérique, scores among the highest-risk streets in the city.

If you're starting out in Paris

Start with the route, not the bike. A route on quieter streets is usually only slightly longer, so try it on a weekend first, then ride it once on a workday. Read the guide: How do I start cycling to work if traffic scares me?

What this score doesn't tell you

The score predicts how badly a crash ends (injury severity), not whether a crash happens.

It was checked against official crash records from major European cities in five countries. That check found the score's signal along streets and not at junctions, where much urban cycling harm happens.

The figures are associations, not causes. Nothing yet shows that changing one rider's route changes their outcome.

A city's score is an average over its whole street network. Risk changes street by street.

Questions about cycling in Paris

Is Paris safer to cycle than Lyon?

The two are too close to separate on this measure, because their likely rank ranges overlap. Paris ranks 13th of 300 with a score of 93.7 (likely 6th–19th), and Lyon ranks 26th with 85.7 (likely 13th–38th). Higher score = lower assessed risk.

What does Paris's score of 93.7 mean?

It places Paris 13th of the 300 European cities in the Pedalability Index 2026. The index runs from 0 to 100 on a fixed scale. Higher score = lower assessed risk. A score of 0.0 means the lowest of the 300 cities, not an absence of safety, and no score means a city is free of risk.

Does the score say how likely a crash is in Paris?

No. The score predicts how badly a crash ends, not whether one happens. It also has no signal at junctions, where much urban cycling harm happens.
