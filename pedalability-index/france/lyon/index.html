Is it safe to cycle in Lyon?

Lyon ranks 26th of 300 European cities in the Pedalability Index 2026, with a score of 85.7 out of 100 (likely rank 13th–38th). Higher score = lower assessed risk. About 10.9% of its street length rates High risk for cyclists, against 7.0% in Montpellier (31st). Risk changes street by street, so the route you choose matters more than the city average.

Lyon is a city of good islands. It ranks 10th in Europe for how many residents can reach daily life on calm streets, but 75th for a typical trip across town, because crossing between those islands still means using the big roads.

If you're starting out in Lyon

Start with the route, not the bike. A route on quieter streets is usually only slightly longer, so try it on a weekend first, then ride it once on a workday. Read the guide: How do I start cycling to work if traffic scares me?

What this score doesn't tell you

The score predicts how badly a crash ends (injury severity), not whether a crash happens.

It was checked against official crash records from major European cities in five countries. That check found the score's signal along streets and not at junctions, where much urban cycling harm happens.

The figures are associations, not causes. Nothing yet shows that changing one rider's route changes their outcome.

A city's score is an average over its whole street network. Risk changes street by street.

Questions about cycling in Lyon

Is Lyon safer to cycle than Montpellier?

The two are too close to separate on this measure, because their likely rank ranges overlap. Lyon ranks 26th of 300 with a score of 85.7 (likely 13th–38th), and Montpellier ranks 31st with 83.6 (likely 27th–46th). Higher score = lower assessed risk.

What does Lyon's score of 85.7 mean?

It places Lyon 26th of the 300 European cities in the Pedalability Index 2026. The index runs from 0 to 100 on a fixed scale. Higher score = lower assessed risk. A score of 0.0 means the lowest of the 300 cities, not an absence of safety, and no score means a city is free of risk.

Does the score say how likely a crash is in Lyon?

No. The score predicts how badly a crash ends, not whether one happens. It also has no signal at junctions, where much urban cycling harm happens.
