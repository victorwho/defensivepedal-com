2. What the service is

Defensive Pedal is a cycling navigation app. It gives every street in its coverage area a risk rating built from more than 100 road-risk factors, plans routes that favour lower-risk streets, explains why a route was chosen, and flags hazards reported by other riders and weather conditions along the way. Core safety routing is free. Some features are part of a paid Premium subscription (section 5).

Coverage currently includes 31 European countries: all EU member states, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Norway and Iceland. The level of detail depends on local map data and is strongest in large cities.

4. Safety: what the app can and cannot do

Route suggestions are advice, not a guarantee. The risk ratings are statistical. They describe how streets compare with each other based on road characteristics, traffic data and crash records. We have checked the model against national crash registers in five countries and found that serious injuries are more common on streets it rates as high risk, but that is an association across many riders and many streets. It says nothing certain about any single ride, and a lower-risk route can still be dangerous on a given day.

By using the app you agree that:

  • You obey traffic law and follow the instructions of the police and road signs, which take precedence over anything the app shows.
  • You keep your eyes on the road. Set up your route before you start and use audio guidance or a handlebar mount rather than handling the phone while riding.
  • You judge conditions yourself. Roadworks, weather, surface damage, traffic and lighting change faster than any map, and our data can be wrong, incomplete or out of date.
  • Hazard reports come from other riders. We do not verify them before they appear, and they may be inaccurate or no longer current.
  • You use suitable equipment for the conditions, including lights and, where required by law, a helmet.
