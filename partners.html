  • Cities with safer streets see fewer serious cycling injuries. Across 18 European cities with bike counters, every 10 index points goes with about 22% fewer riders killed or seriously injured per bike trip.
  • On the streets we rate High risk, serious injuries are more than twice as likely per passage as on the streets we rate Safer, across 2,034 counting sites in five countries.
  • It works where crash data is missing, because it scores the roads themselves. We checked 45 cities against official bike-infrastructure figures: OpenStreetMap was typically close, and adding missing lanes moved no city out of its likely rank range. Small print: the city link is an association between countries, not proof of cause. The street model predicts injury severity, not crash frequency.
