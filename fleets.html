For courier and e-bike fleets in Bucharest

Take your riders off high-risk roads for about 5% more distance.

Riders chasing the fastest route end up on busy arterials, shift after shift. One serious crash can mean an injured rider, a missed shift and a gap in your schedule. The Fleet Route-Risk Audit shows where your riders are exposed and gives them lower-risk routes they can ride tomorrow. On average, our safer routes are about 5% longer than the shortest route.

What the audit gives you

  • A risk map of the streets your fleet rides most, rated Safer, Typical or High risk.
  • The handful of junctions and stretches where most of the risk concentrates.
  • Lower-risk alternatives for your most common trips, with the extra distance each one adds. In 10,149 test trips in Romanian cities, our routes carried lower modelled risk than Google Maps cycling directions on 99 of every 100.
