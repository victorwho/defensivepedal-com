How it works

Potholes, hostile merges and risky junctions are flagged before you reach them, and you’ll get a warning when the weather means gearing up.

Questions

Quick answers, no fine print.

Is Defensive Pedal really free?

Yes. Core safety routing is completely free — every ride, every day. Optional premium adds Cool Routing, e-bike routing, unlimited saved routes and offline navigation, but the safety-first navigation that makes us different is free for everyone.

Will the safer route take much longer?

Not much. On average, our routes are just 5% longer than the shortest route. On a 4 km ride, that’s about 200 metres.

Do I need an account?

Yes. You create a free account when you first open the app.

Is it good for e-bikes and commuting?

Yes. It’s built for everyday city riding, commute included. E-bike routing is part of Premium.

Will it drain my phone battery?

Defensive Pedal is optimised to be lightweight. Battery usage is comparable to other GPS navigation apps. For longer rides we recommend a power bank or handlebar charger — same as you would for any turn-by-turn app.

Does it work in my city?

If you're in Europe, yes — Defensive Pedal is available in 31 countries: every EU country plus the UK, Switzerland, Norway and Iceland. Because we build on OpenStreetMap data, the engine can extend further. The granularity of safety scoring depends on local data quality. Major European cities have the strongest coverage today, and we improve everywhere as our community grows.

Is there an iPhone version?

Yes! Defensive Pedal is now live on the App Store — same safety-first routing as on Android. Thank you to everyone who joined the waitlist and helped us get here.

Download for iPhone

How accurate are the safety scores?

Our safety model uses 100+ road-risk factors per segment, drawing on official European road safety research, OpenStreetMap infrastructure data, and community-reported hazards. The model has been refined with input from 2,400+ contributors and tested across 10,149 routes in Romanian cities — producing routes 2.1x safer than default routing, measured as cumulative modelled risk. We have also checked the model against the official crash registers of five European countries: serious injuries are more common on the roads it rates as high risk.

What data does the app collect?

Privacy by design. We collect only what's needed to route you safely. All analytics are opt-in, GDPR-compliant, and aggregated/anonymised for any institutional data sharing. We never sell personal data.
