How it works

Defensive Pedal finds you the safest route in 3 steps.

01

Risk mapping

We score every road segment using 79+ factors — lane width, speed limit, surface quality, intersection complexity, signalling, dooring zones, and more.

02

Risk routing

When you set a destination, our algorithm analyses every possible route and finds the one with the lowest cumulative risk — not just the shortest.

03

Live hazard alerts

As you ride, the app warns you about upcoming hazards — potholes, hostile merges, dangerous intersections — so you're ready, not surprised.

Questions

Quick answers, no fine print.

Is Defensive Pedal really free?

Yes. Core safety routing is completely free — every ride, every day. Optional premium tiers will unlock advanced features like personalised risk profiles and route analytics, but the safety-first navigation that makes us different is free for everyone.

Will it drain my phone battery?

Defensive Pedal is optimised to be lightweight. Battery usage is comparable to other GPS navigation apps. For longer rides we recommend a power bank or handlebar charger — same as you would for any turn-by-turn app.

Does it work in my city?

Yes — Defensive Pedal works in every city in the world because we build on OpenStreetMap data. That said, the granularity of safety scoring depends on local data quality. Major European cities have the strongest coverage today, and we improve everywhere as our community grows.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet — we're Android-first. iOS is on the roadmap. Join the waitlist below and we'll email you the moment it launches.

Thanks — you're on the list. 🚴

How accurate are the safety scores?

Our safety model uses 79+ road-risk factors per segment, drawing on official European road safety research, OpenStreetMap infrastructure data, and community-reported hazards. The model has been refined with input from 1,300+ contributors and tested across 10,149 routes in Romanian cities — showing routes 110% safer than default routing.

What data does the app collect?

Privacy by design. We collect only what's needed to route you safely. All analytics are opt-in, GDPR-compliant, and aggregated/anonymised for any institutional data sharing. We never sell personal data.
