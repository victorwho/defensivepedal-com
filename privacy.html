Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026 · Applies to the Defensive Pedal app (Android and iOS) and defensivepedal.com

Defensive Pedal is a cycling app that plans bike routes around traffic risk. To do that the app needs to know where you are while you ride. This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, who else sees it, and the rights you have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who is responsible for your data?

The data controller is Antifragil SRL, the company that makes Defensive Pedal, a company registered in Romania (registered office: V. V. Stanciu 5, Bucharest, Romania; trade register no. J2017020730400; tax ID (CUI) 38619226). You can reach us about privacy at privacy@defensivepedal.com. We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer because we are a small company that does not process data on a large scale; the same address reaches the person responsible for data protection.

3. The website

defensivepedal.com uses Umami, a privacy-focused analytics service, to count visits. Umami does not set cookies and does not store personal identifiers; it records the page viewed, the referrer, country, browser and device type. The site is served by Cloudflare, whose servers log your IP address for security and keep those logs briefly. The site loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the font is requested. The site sets no advertising or tracking cookies.

Some providers above are in the United States. When data leaves the EEA we rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and otherwise on the Commission's standard contractual clauses. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards in place.

6. How do we protect your data?

Data travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections (TLS). Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text. Access to production systems is limited to the people who need it to run the service, and precise location data is kept separate from account identifiers wherever a feature allows it. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach ever puts your rights at risk we will tell you and the supervisory authority as the GDPR requires.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correct data that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete your data. You can delete your account in the app under Settings, or write to us.
  • Export your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Restrict or object to processing based on our legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent at any time for analytics (in Settings) and for location (in your phone's settings). Withdrawing does not affect what was done before.

Write to privacy@defensivepedal.com. We answer within one month; for complex requests the GDPR lets us take up to two more months, and we will tell you if that happens. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Romanian supervisory authority, the Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal (ANSPDCP), B-dul G-ral. Gheorghe Magheru 28–30, Sector 1, 010336 Bucharest, dataprotection.ro, or to the data protection authority in the country where you live.

8. Children

Defensive Pedal is for people aged 16 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from younger children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Automated decisions

The app scores streets, not people. Route suggestions are calculated from road and traffic data and are the same for everyone with the same settings. We make no automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

10. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date at the top. For material changes, such as a new category of data or a new purpose, we notify you in the app before the change takes effect.
